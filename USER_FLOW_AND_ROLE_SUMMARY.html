Home2School — Role Architecture & User Flows
Complete Product Specification: Parent, Driver, and Walker (WalkShare)

📌 1. Executive Summary

Home2School is an institutional-grade child mobility and school transit platform engineered to ensure student safety, eliminate parental anxiety, and support flexible neighborhood transportation options. It connects three key roles:

👨‍👩‍👧 Parent

Manages child profiles, chooses Car/Walk service, books recurring or one-off rides, and monitors commute progress with live GPS tracking & safe arrival notifications.

🚗 Driver (Vehicle Ride)

Vetted independent drivers with multi-seat vehicles (Car/Van) offering medium-to-long distance commutes, route sequence optimization, and student check-in/out.

🚶‍♂️ Walker (WalkShare)

Verified neighborhood chaperones leading a "Walking School Bus" for short-distance clusters, walking children safely along sidewalk corridors and zebra crossings.

👨‍👩‍👧 3. Parent User Flow

flowchart LR A[Onboarding / Login] --> B[Add Child Profile] B --> C[Select Service: Ride or Walk] C --> D[Choose Provider & Book] D --> E[Live GPS Tracking & SOS] E --> F[Safe School Arrival Alert]
  • 1
    Authentication & Profile: Fast phone OTP verification, relationship setup (Mother/Father/Guardian), and emergency contacts.
  • 2
    Child Rider Setup: Register child name, photo, school name, grade, dismissal hours, and preset pickup locations.
  • 3
    Booking & Provider Selection: Choose Vehicle Ride or WalkShare, select recurring days (Mon–Fri), and inspect provider verification badges & ratings.
  • 4
    Live Tracking & Safety: Real-time map location, status chips ("On the way", "Boarded", "Arrived Safely"), and instant call/chat.

🚗 4. Driver User Flow

flowchart LR A[Driver KYC & License] --> B[Set Vehicle & Seats] B --> C[Receive & Accept Requests] C --> D[Navigate Pickup Route] D --> E[Check-in Child & Drop-off] E --> F[Earnings & Rating Scorecard]
  • 1
    Onboarding & Verification: Upload Driving License, Vehicle Fitness, Insurance, and Police Background Clearance.
  • 2
    Seat & Schedule Management: Set total available seats and morning/afternoon operating school zones.
  • 3
    Trip Execution: Turn-by-turn multi-stop route navigation, 1-tap "Child Boarded" attendance check-in, and school drop-off.
  • 4
    Earnings & Reviews: Transparent daily/weekly revenue balance and parent feedback scorecards.

🚶‍♂️ 5. Walker (WalkShare) User Flow

flowchart LR A[Walker Verification] --> B[Define Walking Corridor] B --> C[Accept Neighborhood Kids] C --> D[Morning Muster & Roll Call] D --> E[Supervised Safe Walk] E --> F[School Gate Handover]
  • 1
    Chaperone Verification: Identity & community safety clearance and child safety guideline briefing.
  • 2
    Safe Walking Corridor: Designate low-traffic sidewalk routes and zebra crossing checkpoints.
  • 3
    Escorted Morning Walk: Wear high-vis safety gear, gather children at muster points, and perform digital roll-call.
  • 4
    Gate Handover: Hand children directly over to school duty staff and broadcast "Safe Arrival" notification to parents.
