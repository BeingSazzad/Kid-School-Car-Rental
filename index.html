FAQ

How are drivers vetted?
Vulnerable-sector check, driving abstract, vehicle inspection, and orientation before any student rides.
Can I track rides live?
Yes — live location and ETAs during active trips, plus pickup and drop-off alerts.
Multiple children, different schools?
Select every child in trip setup. Multi-child pricing is arranged with the provider — no auto sibling discount.
What if the driver is late?
Delays over 5 minutes trigger parent SMS and push alerts.
Pause during school breaks?
Pause or cancel from Bookings with notice to your provider. Platform subscription is separate — no ride wallet credits.
Platform fee vs ride fee?
Home2School bills the subscription. Ride pay stays between you and the provider after they accept.
