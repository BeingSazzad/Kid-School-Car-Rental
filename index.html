Help & FAQ

How are drivers background-checked and vetted?
Every Home2School driver passes a multi-tier vulnerable sector criminal record check, clean driving abstract verification, annual vehicle inspection, and in-person orientation before accepting student rides.
Can parents track child rides with live GPS?
Yes! During active school commutes, parents receive live telemetry showing vehicle position, estimated arrival times, driver status, and instant push notifications upon pickup and safe school drop-off.
Can I book for multiple children attending different schools?
Yes! In Step 1, select all children traveling. The system matches you with neighborhood providers certified for sequential drop-off routes with multi-child sibling discounts automatically applied.
What happens if a driver is delayed in morning traffic?
If a driver encounters delays beyond 5 minutes, our automated tracking algorithm immediately alerts parents via SMS and push notification, and standby dispatch is notified.
Can I pause or cancel during school vacations?
Absolutely. You can pause recurring bookings from the Bookings tab up to 24 hours in advance with zero penalty fees, and unused days remain as credit in your In-App Wallet.
How are multi-child sibling discounts applied?
When booking for 2 or more children on the same commute route, a $10–$20 weekly sibling discount is automatically deducted directly on the Booking Summary invoice.

What is Home2School?

Home2School is a specialized, safety-certified child transportation and school commute platform designed specifically for busy families. It gives parents a reliable, stress-free alternative to crowded buses and hectic morning drop-offs.

Whether you need daily round-trip commutes, morning-only drop-offs, or a neighborhood walking school escort, Home2School matches your family with background-checked community drivers who treat your children like family.

How It Works for Parents
1
Register Child Profiles
Add school names, dismissal schedules, booster seat requirements, and teacher hand-off notes.
2
Set Commute Schedule
Choose recurring weekly days (Mon–Fri) or one-time rides, morning pickup times, and return dismissal times.
3
Match with Verified Drivers
Review licensed neighborhood drivers with background checks, ratings, vehicle capacity, and sibling discounts.
4
Live GPS & Safe Gate Hand-Off
Track the vehicle in real-time, get instant departure/arrival alerts, and communicate directly with your driver.
Our Mission & Promise

Home2School was created by parents to eliminate daily school commute anxiety. We connect neighborhood families with certified, community-verified drivers and walking escorts to make morning drop-offs and afternoon pickups safe, peaceful, and predictable.
